**# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard | Excel**
An interactive **\*\*Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard\*\*** built in **\*\*Microsoft Excel 2021\*\*** to transform patient and emergency-room data into clear, business-focused insights.
\---
**## 📌 Project Overview**
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of emergency-room performance across different years and months.
**### The dashboard analyzes**
\- 👥 Patient volume
\- ⏱️ Average patient wait time
\- ⭐ Patient satisfaction
\- 🏥 Admission status
\- 🚨 Patients attended within time vs. delayed
\- 👤 Gender distribution
\- 👶 Age-group distribution
\- 📍 Department referrals
\- ⏳ Average wait time by department
\- 📈 Month-over-month performance changes
The goal was not simply to create charts, but to build an **\*\*interactive analytical dashboard\*\*** that makes operational and patient-related patterns easier to explore and understand.
\---
**## 🎯 Business Problem**
Emergency departments generate large amounts of patient data. Looking at individual records makes it difficult to quickly understand:
\- How many patients are being handled?
\- Are patients waiting too long?
\- Are patients being attended within the expected time?
\- What proportion of patients are admitted?
\- Which departments receive more referrals?
\- Which departments have relatively higher waiting times?
\- How are patient volume, waiting time, and satisfaction changing over time?
This dashboard brings these questions together in one interactive Excel report.
\---

**# 🔎 Key Insights**
The February 2024 dashboard view revealed several important patterns.
**### 👥 Patient Volume**
\- Patient activity was lower compared with the previous month.
\- The month-over-month KPI showed a noticeable decline in patient volume.
**### ⏱️ Waiting Time**
\- Average waiting time increased slightly compared with the previous month.
\- A reduction in patient volume did not automatically result in a reduction in waiting time.
\- Some departments showed noticeably higher average waiting times than others.
**### ⭐ Patient Satisfaction**
\- Patient satisfaction declined compared with the previous month.
\- Satisfaction is therefore an important KPI to monitor alongside waiting time and patient volume.
**### 🚨 Attendance Performance**
\- A larger share of patients experienced delays than were attended within the expected time.
\- Attendance performance may require further investigation.
**### 🏥 Admission Status**
\- The admitted and non-admitted patient groups were relatively balanced.
\- Admission performance can be monitored alongside patient volume and attendance patterns.
**### 👥 Demographics**
\- Female patients represented a slightly larger share of the selected month's patient population.
\- Patient volume varied across the different age categories.
**### 📍 Department Referrals**
\- \`None\` was the largest referral category.
\- General Practice was one of the more prominent named referral categories.
\- Orthopedics also represented a significant share of referrals.
**### ⏳ Department Waiting Times**
\- Physiotherapy recorded the highest displayed average waiting time in the selected February 2024 view.
\- Comparing departments helps identify where further operational analysis may be useful.
**### 💡 Overall Business Observation**
The dashboard demonstrates that **\*\*patient volume, waiting time, satisfaction, and attendance performance need to be viewed together\*\***.
A decrease in patient volume does not necessarily mean better operational performance. Monitoring multiple KPIs together provides a more complete picture.
\> **\*\*Note:\*\*** This section intentionally focuses on analytical findings and patterns rather than listing dashboard values.

**# 📈 Month-over-Month Analysis**
The dashboard compares the selected month with the previous month for:
\- Patient count
\- Average wait time
\- Patient satisfaction
**### Comparison Logic**
\- 🟢 Positive movement is shown using a green upward indicator.
\- 🔴 Negative movement is shown using a red downward indicator.
\- January requires special handling because there is no immediately preceding month within the selected year's monthly sequence.
\- For the beginning of 2024, December 2023 can serve as the previous calendar month where applicable.
