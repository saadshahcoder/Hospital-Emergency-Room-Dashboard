# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard | Excel

An interactive **Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Excel 2021** to transform patient and emergency-room data into clear, business-focused insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of emergency-room performance across different years and months.

### The dashboard analyzes

- 👥 Patient volume
- ⏱️ Average patient wait time
- ⭐ Patient satisfaction
- 🏥 Admission status
- 🚨 Patients attended within time vs. delayed
- 👤 Gender distribution
- 👶 Age-group distribution
- 📍 Department referrals
- ⏳ Average wait time by department
- 📈 Month-over-month performance changes

The goal was to build an **interactive analytical dashboard** that makes operational and patient-related patterns easier to explore and understand.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Emergency departments generate large amounts of patient data. Looking at individual records makes it difficult to quickly understand:

- How many patients are being handled?
- Are patients waiting too long?
- Are patients being attended within the expected time?
- What proportion of patients are admitted?
- Which departments receive more referrals?
- Which departments have relatively higher waiting times?
- How are patient volume, waiting time, and satisfaction changing over time?

This dashboard brings these questions together in one interactive Excel report.

## 🔎 Key Insights

The selected dashboard view revealed several important patterns:

- 👥 Patient activity was lower compared with the previous month.
- ⏱️ Average waiting time increased slightly despite lower patient volume.
- ⭐ Patient satisfaction declined compared with the previous month.
- 🚨 A larger share of patients experienced delays than were attended within the expected time.
- 🏥 Admitted and non-admitted patients were relatively balanced.
- 👤 Female patients represented a slightly larger share of the selected patient population.
- 👶 Patient volume varied across different age groups.
- 📍 `None` was the largest referral category, while General Practice and Orthopedics were prominent named referrals.
- ⏳ Physiotherapy showed the highest displayed average waiting time among departments in the selected view.
- 💡 Patient volume, waiting time, satisfaction, and attendance performance should be evaluated together rather than individually.

> **Note:** Key insights intentionally focus on patterns rather than displaying raw dashboard numbers.

## 📌 Important Notes

- Dashboard values change according to the selected Year and Month.
- Key insights focus on patterns rather than displaying all underlying numerical values.
- Month-over-month comparisons depend on the availability of the previous calendar month.
- Department referral categories such as `None` are retained from the underlying dataset.
- This is a **portfolio/learning project** and is not intended to represent real hospital operational reporting.
